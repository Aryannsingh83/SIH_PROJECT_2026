## Overview

**Krishi Setu** is an AgriTech prototype developed for Smart India Hackathon 2026, designed to improve transparency, coordination, and accessibility in agricultural procurement.

It aims to connect farmers, procurement officials, and transportation services through a unified digital workflow.

Our goal is to make the procurement journey easier to monitor — from crop submission and inspection to collection and payment tracking.

## The Problem

Farmers can face several challenges during the procurement process:

- Uncertainty regarding crop inspection schedules.
- Limited visibility into crop quality assessment and weight verification.
- Delays and communication gaps during crop collection.
- Difficulty tracking transportation and payment status.
- Fragmented coordination between farmers and procurement authorities.

## Our Approach

Krishi Setu proposes a connected procurement system that brings these stages together in one place.

| Challenge | Proposed approach |
|---|---|
| Inspection uncertainty | Inspection scheduling and status updates |
| Quality assessment | AI-assisted crop-quality evaluation |
| Weight verification | Digital recording of inspected quantities |
| Collection delays | Collection status tracking |
| Transport coordination | Location and logistics integration |
| Payment uncertainty | Procurement and payment-status visibility |
